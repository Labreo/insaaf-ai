The complainant purchased the vehicle under tax invoice dated 14 October 2025 for Rs. 14,20,000 and paid through bank loan EMI.
Job cards dated 03 December 2025, 19 January 2026 and 02 April 2026 record the same gearbox defect within the warranty period.
The complainant raised a complaint to the seller by email and customer care ticket number CM-77821.
The dealer replied that the defect was due to driving habits, which the complainant disputes.
An independent expert report dated 10 May 2026 confirms a manufacturing defect in the transmission.
