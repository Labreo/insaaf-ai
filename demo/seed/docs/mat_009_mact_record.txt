FIR No. 112/2026 was registered at Margao Town police station on 09 July 2026 against the bus driver for rash and negligent driving.
Medical records and a discharge summary from Goa Medical College show compound fracture of the right femur.
A disability certificate issued by the medical board assesses 40% permanent disability of the right lower limb.
The insurance policy of the offending bus with Oriental Insurance was valid on the date of accident.
The claimant was a student; income proof of the parents is filed.
