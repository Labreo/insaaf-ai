Cheque No. 004512 dated 02 August 2026 for Rs. 6,80,000 drawn on Bank of India, Margao was returned unpaid with the bank return memo stating funds insufficient on 22 August 2026.
The complainant sent a statutory demand notice under section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act by speed post on 05 September 2026, within 30 days of the dishonour memo.
Postal tracking report shows the notice was delivered to the drawer on 08 September 2026.
The cheque was issued towards a legally enforceable debt for TMT bars supplied under tax invoices dated June 2026.
No payment was made within 15 days of receipt of the notice and the cause of action arose on 23 September 2026.
